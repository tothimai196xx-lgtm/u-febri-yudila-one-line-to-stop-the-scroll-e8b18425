An entry for the [One Line to Stop the Scroll](https://make.fun/bounty/u-febri-yudila-one-line-to-stop-the-scroll-e8b18425) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/): one line of text, at most 200 characters, that makes a stranger stop scrolling and smile.

## The entry

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (191 characters, one line, no trailing newline):

> My dog is a tiny manager: every night at nine he brings me his leash, files the paperwork for our walk, and stares at me, because in seven years I have never once been the one who is on time.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It prints the character count (the gate requires 1–200 characters) and writes `/output/entry.txt`.
